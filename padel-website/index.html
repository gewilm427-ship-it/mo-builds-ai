Fix the problem.

Common problems and what to focus on first.

Why does my bandeja sit up?

Reduce the size of your swing. Contact the ball high and slightly in front. Think about controlling the lob and keeping the net instead of trying to hit a winner.

Why do my volleys hit the net?

Keep your racket in front of your body and shorten the backswing. Let a small forward step carry the shot instead of chopping down with your arm.

How do I defend the back glass?

Don't stand too close to the bounce. Give the ball space to travel into the glass, read the rebound and then move your feet.

What should I practise first?

Pick one observable change at a time. Start with preparation, then contact point, then recovery position.
